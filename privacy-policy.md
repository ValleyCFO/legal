# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 12, 2026**

Valley CFO Inc. ("Valley CFO," "we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of our clients and the security of the financial data we work with. This Privacy Policy describes how Valley CFO collects, uses, stores, and protects information accessed through the Valley CFO Reporting application (the "Application"), which integrates with QuickBooks Online ("QBO") via Intuit's developer API.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Application — a Valley CFO–internal tool used by firm staff to generate monthly financial reports for Valley CFO bookkeeping and advisory clients. The Application is not made available to the general public, is not distributed through the QuickBooks App Store, and is not licensed to third parties.

Valley CFO's general firm privacy practices, applicable to all engagements regardless of technology, are governed by our client engagement letters and the AICPA Code of Professional Conduct.

## 2. Information We Access

When authorized by a QuickBooks Online company administrator or accountant user, the Application accesses the following data through Intuit's API:

- Profit and Loss reports (year-to-date and trailing twelve-month)
- Balance Sheet reports (current and prior period)
- Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable aging reports (where applicable)
- Company name, fiscal year start, and reporting basis (cash or accrual)

The Application does **not** access bank credentials, social security numbers, employee personally identifiable information, customer or vendor contact details beyond what is necessary for aging summaries, or any payment-method information.

## 3. How We Use Information

Accessed financial data is used exclusively to:

- Generate monthly branded financial reports delivered to the client
- Compute summary metrics and key performance indicators for advisory purposes
- Construct an indirect-method cash flow statement
- Draft plain-language insights for client review by Valley CFO professional staff

Data is not used for marketing, not sold, and not shared with any third party except as required by law or as expressly authorized by the client.

## 4. Storage and Security

- OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in encrypted form on Valley CFO–controlled storage (Microsoft OneDrive for Business, with tenant-level encryption at rest and in transit).
- Financial data fetched at report-generation time is processed in memory and written to client-specific folders within Valley CFO's Microsoft 365 tenant.
- Access to the Application and to stored credentials is limited to Valley CFO personnel with a legitimate business need.
- Tokens are rotated on each use per Intuit's OAuth 2.0 specification.

## 5. Retention

Generated reports are retained for the duration of the client engagement plus the period required by applicable record-retention rules (generally seven years for accounting and tax records). OAuth tokens are deleted upon written request from the client or upon termination of the engagement.

## 6. Client Rights

A client may at any time:

- Revoke the Application's access through their QuickBooks Online account settings (Settings → Apps → Connected Apps)
- Request a copy of any data Valley CFO holds about them
- Request deletion of stored data, subject to professional record-retention obligations

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 8.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The Application interacts with Intuit's QuickBooks Online platform. Intuit's own privacy practices govern data while it is on Intuit's systems. See Intuit's privacy policy at https://www.intuit.com/privacy/.

The Application does not transmit data to any other third-party service.
